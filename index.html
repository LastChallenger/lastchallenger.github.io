About me

I received my M.Sc. in Computer Control & Automation from Nanyang Technological University in July 2026, and my B.Eng. from Central South University in 2023.

I am seeking Ph.D. opportunities for Fall 2027.

My background is in behavior foundation models, world models, robot dynamics, and reinforcement learning. My research interests are loco-manipulation and whole-body intelligence.

Contact: fanyudong2002@163.com.

Past Research Internships

Mondo Robotics
I worked on locomotion, behavior foundation models (BFMs), and AMP across different humanoid embodiments, covering policy training, motion imitation, and sim-to-real tuning and deployment. I also built teleoperation data-collection workflows, improved wrist tracking, generated ALIP stair motions, and assisted with MPPI trajectory optimization.
Shanghai Qi Zhi Institute
I worked on dynamics-informed reinforcement learning for humanoid control, including full-order and centroidal dynamics, MuJoCo sim-to-sim validation, and policy deployment on Unitree G1. I also worked on LiDAR point-cloud preprocessing, elevation mapping, and sim-to-real experiments for terrain-aware locomotion.
